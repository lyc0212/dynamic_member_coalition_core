```python
while not env.done:

    nominal_evidence = decision_evidence(
        view,
        nominal_choice,
        env.beliefs.accepted_receipts

    result = env.step(
        submitted_assignment,
        checkpoint["checkpoint_id"],
        tick,
        unique_request_id
```

## 22. 实际收费分配变化次数及费用

单位费用：

```text
lambda_0 = 0.01
```

当前源码按每架无人机完整Assignment变化计费。

对每个实际收费边界：

```python
n_k = sum(
    before_i != after_i
    for before_i, after_i in zip(
        actual_before,
        submitted_assignment
    )
)

c_k = lambda_0 * n_k
```
